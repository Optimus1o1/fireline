## 1. Primary NASA Flight Investigation Repositories

1. **NASA Physical Sciences Informatics (PSI) System**  
   National Aeronautics and Space Administration.  
   URL: [https://psi.nasa.gov/](https://psi.nasa.gov/)

2. **Saffire-I Investigation (PSI-98)**  
   *Spacecraft Fire Safety Demonstration Project - Flight 1*  
   NASA PSI Investigation Accession: `PSI-98`  
   Artifact Identifier: `PSI98-ART-002` (Specimen metadata, flow duct instrumentation, and test conditions)  
   Platform: Orbital ATK Cygnus Pressurized Cargo Module (OA-6 Mission)  
   Primary Investigator: Dr. David L. Urban, NASA Glenn Research Center  
   URL: [https://psi.nasa.gov/investigation/Saffire-I](https://psi.nasa.gov/investigation/Saffire-I)

3. **BASS-II Investigation (PSI-25)**  
   *Burning and Suppression of Solids - II*  
   NASA PSI Investigation Accession: `PSI-25`  
   Artifact Identifier: `PSI25-ART-002` (PMMA thin-film combustion records, fan indices, and extinction limits)  
   Platform: International Space Station (ISS) Microgravity Science Glovebox (MSG)  
   Primary Investigator: Dr. Sandra L. Olson & Dr. Paul V. Ferkul, NASA Glenn Research Center  
   URL: [https://psi.nasa.gov/investigation/BASS-II](https://psi.nasa.gov/investigation/BASS-II)

4. **FLEX Investigation (PSI-69)**  
   *Flame Extinction Experiment*  
   NASA PSI Investigation Accession: `PSI-69`  
   Platform: International Space Station (ISS) Combustion Integrated Rack (CIR)  
   Primary Investigator: Dr. Forman A. Williams, Dr. Vedha Nayagam, Dr. Daniel L. Dietrich  
   URL: [https://psi.nasa.gov/investigation/FLEX](https://psi.nasa.gov/investigation/FLEX)

## 2. Peer-Reviewed Academic Literature

   *Flame spread in large-scale microgravity experiments: Results from Saffire-I, II, and III.*  
   **Combustion and Flame**, 205, 371–382.  
   DOI: [10.1016/j.combustflame.2019.04.015](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.combustflame.2019.04.015)

2. **Olson, S. L., & Ferkul, P. V.** (2017).  
   *Microgravity flame spread over PMMA thin films: The Burning and Suppression of Solids-II (BASS-II) experiment.*  
   **NASA Technical Memorandum**, NASA/TM-2017-219504. NASA Glenn Research Center, Cleveland, OH.  
   Accession: [NASA Technical Reports Server (NTRS)](https://ntrs.nasa.gov/citations/20170005471)

3. **Nayagam, V., Dietrich, D. L., Ferkul, P. V., Hicks, M. C., & Williams, F. A.** (2015).  
   *Cool-flame extinction in droplet combustion under microgravity.*  
   **Combustion and Flame**, 162(5), 2140–2147.  
   DOI: [10.1016/j.combustflame.2015.01.012](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.combustflame.2015.01.012)

4. **T'ien, J. S., Shih, H. Y., & Jiang, C. B.** (2001).

5. **Dietrich, D. L., Nayagam, V., Hicks, M. C., Ferkul, P. V., Dryer, F. L., Farouk, T. I., Shaw, B. D., Suh, H. K., & Williams, F. A.** (2014).
